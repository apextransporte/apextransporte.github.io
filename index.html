Key rules

Keep the server safe and friendly for every driver. Read these five first.

  1. 1

    Respect everyone

    No hate speech, personal attacks or arguments over politics. English is the main language.

  2. 2

    Keep it clean

    No NSFW content, malware or pirated software. Never share personal details.

  3. 3

    One account only

    No alt or fake accounts. Do not impersonate staff.

  4. 4

    No mods, no sharing skins

    Modded game clients mean a ban. Company skins stay with official members.

  5. 5

    Follow staff

    Respect staff decisions. Report problems to a Moderator and do not confront others in public.

Promotion ladder

Your kilometers earn your rank. Every job you log moves you closer to Legend.

  1. Rookie

    0 km

    No reward

  2. Driver

    5,000 km

    500 coins

  3. Road Pro

    25,000 km

    1,000 coins

  4. Senior Driver

    75,000 km

    2,500 coins

  5. Elite

    150,000 km

    5,000 coins

  6. Legend

    300,000 km

    10,000 coins

How it works

The bot reads your total km from our stats and gives you the matching rank role. You do not need to ask. Your new rank shows a few minutes after your job card appears.

Rank rules

  • Each rank pays coins one time only.
  • You hold one rank role at a time.
  • If one job moves you up two ranks, you get both rewards.
  • The bot never lowers your rank.
  • Only Official members get ranks.

Check your progress

Type /myrank in any channel. Only you see the answer. It shows your rank, your total km and the km left for the next rank.

Tips

  • Log every job with a clear screenshot in job-logs.
  • Keep your Discord name close to your name in the stats.
  • Tell a staff member if your rank looks wrong.

FAQ

Quick answers before you join.

Which game do you play?

We drive in Truckers of Europe 3. Our routes cover Türkiye and Continental Europe. Pick a truck, take a job, and post your result in the Discord job logs channel.

Is there an age limit?

You need a Discord account, so you must meet Discord's minimum age. Drivers of all ages are welcome. Every member follows the server rules and treats others with respect. Read the rules before you join.

How fast do staff reply?

Staff reply in 24 hours or less. Most answers arrive sooner. Write your question in the Discord server and wait for a staff member. Send it once and keep the chat open.
